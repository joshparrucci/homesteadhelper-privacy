HomesteadHelper privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Publisher: HomesteadHelper
Privacy contact: parrucci.j.s@gmail.com

Records stored on your device

HomesteadHelper is a garden and homestead management app. Your settings, property and garden boundaries, tasks, inventory, watering, spraying, chicken and journal records, and selected photos are stored locally on your device. The current release does not require an app account, display advertising, include app-managed analytics or remote crash reporting, or automatically upload your records to a HomesteadHelper server.

Optional location access

If you choose a feature that uses your current location, the app requests foreground location permission to centre the map or set a weather location. You can instead position the map or choose a location manually. The app does not request background location access or track your movements in the background. Saved locations and boundaries can reveal where your property is.

Online maps — Esri ArcGIS

Opening or moving an online map requests map styles, tiles and related resources directly from Esri ArcGIS services. These requests disclose your IP address, the requested map area and normal network request information to the provider. HomesteadHelper does not upload your traced polygons, photos or garden records to Esri. Map style metadata is cached briefly in memory; map rendering may also use the mapping library’s device cache. The app does not offer offline imagery downloads. Esri handles service requests under its own privacy policy: https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview.

Weather — WeatherAPI.com

When a weather feature loads a forecast for a location you have chosen, coordinates rounded to three decimal places are sent directly to WeatherAPI.com. The provider also receives your IP address and normal network request information. Forecast data is cached temporarily in app memory and reused between weather screens; it is not included in your garden backups. No weather request is made until a weather location has been chosen. Provider processing and retention are governed by its privacy policy: https://www.weatherapi.com/privacy.aspx.

Photos and reminders

You may choose individual photos using the system photo picker for supported records. The app processes and stores selected copies locally and does not request broad access to your photo library, camera or microphone. If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. Reminder details may appear on your lock screen, depending on your Android settings. You can change permissions and notification visibility in Android settings.

Backups and sharing

Backup and restore are actions you initiate. Exported files can contain your records, property coordinates, boundaries and photos. The app does not add password protection or encryption to these backup files. They go to the destination or app you select in the system save/share flow. A selected cloud, email or messaging provider handles that copy under its own terms and privacy policy. Keep exports private and use a secure destination. HomesteadHelper cannot access or delete copies you save elsewhere. The Android release disables the app’s standard automatic Android backup.

Retention, deletion and security

Local records remain until you delete them using available app controls, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes locally stored records and settings but does not delete exported backups or original photos in your photo library. Remove those copies separately if wanted. Online providers control retention of their service logs; clearing app storage does not erase those logs. Map and weather requests use HTTPS. Local records and manual backup files are not protected by an additional app password or app-level encryption; protect your device and backups. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Privacy questions and requests

HomesteadHelper is the publisher and privacy contact for this app. Email parrucci.j.s@gmail.com with questions, complaints or requests about your information. If you contact us, your email address, message and any attachments are handled through our email provider to respond and resolve the request, and retained as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Do not email backup files, exact property locations or other sensitive records unless necessary. We cannot retrieve records stored only on your device. Online map, weather and email providers may process information outside Australia under their own policies.

Scope and policy changes

This policy covers the current HomesteadHelper Android release. Google Play separately processes paid app purchases, app distribution and platform information under Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Satellite imagery is included in the app; there is no separate imagery purchase or subscription. Before adding in-app purchases, account services or other changes to data handling, we will update this policy and provide any required notices or consent. We do not sell your personal information.
